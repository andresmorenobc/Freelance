Project management

How I manage motion projects

A clear structure from brief to delivery, so the project has space for creative work without losing control of scope, formats or feedback.

01

Brief and direction

Understand the goal, audience, message, constraints, formats and desired perception.

02

Moodboard and styleframes

Define the visual universe, references, rhythm, color, typography and composition.

03

Storyboard and production plan

Structure the narrative, scenes, timing, deliverables, review process and production schedule.

04

Motion production

Produce the 2D/3D animation, editing, sound, compositing, transitions and versions.

05

Review and delivery

Manage feedback, revisions, final exports, formats and optimized delivery files.

Structured enough to be reliable. Flexible enough to find the unexpected.
