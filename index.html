FAQ

Common questions.

What kind of motion design projects do you create?

SaaS explainers, 3D product visuals, brand films, campaign motion systems, social motion ads and motion identity assets.

Can you manage a project from concept to final delivery?

Yes — from brief, visual direction and styleframes to storyboard, animation, revisions and final exports.

Do you work with agencies and startups?

Yes — agencies, startups, SaaS companies, creative teams and brands that need motion design support.

Can you create both 2D and 3D motion?

Yes — 2D motion, kinetic typography, UI animation, 3D visualization, compositing and editing.

Can you create versions for social media and paid campaigns?

Yes — horizontal, vertical and square formats, plus shorter cutdowns for social and paid media.

Do you use AI in your creative workflow?

Yes, when useful — AI-assisted workflows for visual exploration, styleframes and lookdev, always guided by art direction.

How long does a motion project take?

Simple assets take a few days. SaaS explainers, brand films or 3D videos need more time depending on scope, feedback and complexity.

How can I get an estimate?

Use the project calculator for a first range, then share your brief or references for a precise quote.
