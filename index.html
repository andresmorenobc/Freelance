Project management

How I manage motion projects

A clear structure from brief to delivery, so the project has space for creative work without losing control of scope, formats or feedback.

01

Brief and direction

Understand the goal, audience, message, references, constraints, formats and desired perception.

02

Visual direction

Define moodboards, styleframes, rhythm, typography, color, composition and motion language.

03

Storyboard and production plan

Structure the narrative, scenes, timing, deliverables, review process and production schedule.

04

Motion production

Produce 2D animation, 3D visuals, editing, sound rhythm, VFX, compositing and format versions.

05

Review and delivery

Manage feedback, revisions, final exports, optimized files and delivery formats.

Structured enough to be reliable. Flexible enough to find the unexpected.
